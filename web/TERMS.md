## 2. Interactive Terminal Authorization Token

1. **Physical Token Role:** The WebDeck serves as a physical authorization terminal. Tapping **"Allow"** on an incoming tool approval request instructs your workstation daemon to immediately execute the displayed terminal command.
2. **Duty of Diligence:** You must inspect all terminal commands, scripts, and arguments displayed on your mobile screen before authorizing them. You assume full legal, operational, and technical responsibility for all actions executed by your coding agents.
3. **No Warranty for Unmonitored Sessions:** While OverWatch implements a strict 20-second timeout block (fail-closed law), you should not leave the web companion connected without human supervision.

## 3. Screen Wake Lock & Device Ergonomics

* The WebDeck utilizes `navigator.wakeLock` to prevent your phone display from sleeping while resting on a MagSafe desk stand.
* You acknowledge that keeping a smartphone display illuminated for extended periods consumes battery power and may generate moderate heat depending on ambient temperature. It is recommended to use an external charging stand.
* OverWatch is not liable for device battery degradation, screen burn-in, or hardware wear resulting from continuous display usage.
